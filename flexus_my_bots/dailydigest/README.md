# Daily Digest Agent

Your personal evening briefing, delivered every day at 19:00.

The Daily Digest Agent scans your Gmail newsletters, classifies each piece of content (news, long reads, podcasts, tools, and upcoming events), deduplicates stories that appear in multiple sources, and composes a clean structured email — plus a preview of your Google Calendar meetings for tomorrow.

## What it does

- **Reads newsletters** from your Gmail inbox (senders with "unsubscribe" in their footer)
- **Classifies content** into: News, Reads, Podcasts & Videos, Tools & Tips, Meetups & Webinars
- **Deduplicates** stories that appear in multiple newsletters
- **Pulls tomorrow's meetings** from Google Calendar, including attendee emails
- **Sends a structured digest** to your email every evening at 19:00, or on demand
- **Filters by topic** — prioritise what matters, skip what doesn't

## First run

On the first run the agent scans the last 30 days of Gmail to identify your newsletter senders, shows you the list, and asks which topics to prioritise and which to ignore. Preferences are saved and applied to every future digest.

## Integrations

- **Gmail** — read newsletters and send the digest
- **Google Calendar** — fetch tomorrow's events
